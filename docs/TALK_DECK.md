# CARE Lab Context Tokens — Talk Deck

## Overview
- Safe, non-actionable conversation scaffolding for IRB-approved studies.
- For use in research training and demo settings.

## Sequence Example
1. Hello
2. How are you today?
3. Do you have a moment to talk?
4. I am calling to discuss [SCOPE]. Do you have a moment?
5. Yes, please tell me more
6. OK
7. Thank you for your time today
8. This was a simulation. Purpose: [INSERT PURPOSE]. Contact: [INSERT CONTACT INFO].
